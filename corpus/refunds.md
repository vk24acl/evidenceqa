# Northstar demo refund policy
This is a fictional policy written for the EvidenceQA portfolio demo.

## Refund window
Refund requests are accepted within 14 calendar days after purchase.
Customers must include the order number and the purchase email in a refund request.
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## Exceptions
Custom consulting services are not eligible for refunds once delivery has started.
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, but cancellation does not automatically create a refund.
