# Northstar demo support handbook
This fictional handbook is part of a demonstration, not a real service commitment.

## Support hours
Customer support operates Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC.
Urgent incident reports use the incident form and receive priority over general support questions.

## Response targets
The target first response time for standard support tickets is 8 working hours.
Enterprise support tickets have a target first response time of 2 working hours.
